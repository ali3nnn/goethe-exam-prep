Free, browser-based practice for the Goethe-Zertifikat German exams.

## What's inside (A1)

Three complete practice exams: the official **Modellsatz** plus **Übungssatz 01** and **Übungssatz 02**. Pick one at the top of the page. Each set keeps its own progress and results.

All four modules of the exam:

- **Hören**: short conversations, announcements and phone messages, with real audio
- **Lesen**: messages, websites and signs
- **Schreiben**: fill in a form and write a short message
- **Sprechen**: introduce yourself, ask and answer questions, make requests. The page plays the examiner with text-to-speech, and you rate yourself.

There are two modes:

- **Prüfung simulieren** (exam simulation): timed modules, audio limited to the real number of plays, and a scored result sheet at the end (pass mark 60/100).
- **Nur üben** (practice): no timer, replay audio as often as you want, and check each part immediately.

Everything runs in your browser. Progress is saved locally on your device, and there is no sign-up or tracking.

## Run it locally

Download or clone the repo and open `A1/index.html` in any modern browser. Keep the `A1/audio` folder next to it for the listening tasks. It works offline, apart from the web font.
